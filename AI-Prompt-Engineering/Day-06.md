
# Day 06 – AI Agents and Automation

## Learning Objective
Understand AI agents and learn how automation can simplify business tasks.

## What Are AI Agents?
AI agents are systems that can use AI to perform tasks, make decisions within defined instructions, and work toward specific goals.

## Practical Assignment
Client: Smart Digital Agency

Task: Design a simple AI-powered customer support workflow.

## Automation Workflow

1. Customer sends a message.
2. AI reads and understands the message.
3. AI identifies the customer's question.
4. AI prepares an appropriate response.
5. Human staff reviews important or sensitive requests.

## Example Business Applications
- Customer support
- Email organization
- Social media content planning
- Data entry
- Appointment management

## Skills Practised
- AI agents fundamentals
- Workflow design
- Automation concepts
- Problem solving
- Business process improvement

I learned the basic concepts of AI agents and how automation can improve business workflows.
