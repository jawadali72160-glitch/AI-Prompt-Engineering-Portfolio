
# Day 04 – Social Media Video Scripting

## Learning Objective
Learn how to create engaging short-form video scripts using AI.

## Practical Assignment
Client: TechFuture Academy

Target Audience:
Students, beginners and job seekers.

Topic:
5 Free AI Tools Every Beginner Should Know.

## Video Requirements
- Platform: Instagram Reels
- Duration: 30–40 seconds
- Format: Vertical 9:16
- Language: Simple English
- Hook: Capture attention in the first 2 seconds.

## Script Structure
1. Attention-grabbing hook
2. Introduction
3. Main information
4. Call to action

## Skills Practised
- Video scripting
- Audience research
- AI-assisted content creation
- Social media strategy
- Marketing communication

## Learning Outcome
I learned how to structure short videos with engaging hooks, useful information and clear calls to action.
